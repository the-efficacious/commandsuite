`objectives_list` no longer calls its result "your whole open plate". The tool queries the relationship union (`related`), while the plate the runner's `context_refresh` re-brief pushes is assignee-scoped — the two differ for anyone who originates or watches work, and an agent recovering from a restart was being taught both sets under one word, in the tool description and in its standing instruction packet at once. "Plate" now names only what `assignee` selects.
