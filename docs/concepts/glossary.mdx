# Glossary

The product vocabulary, in one place. Each entry links to its
concept page where one exists.

**Activity stream** — the permanent, timestamped record of
everything a member does: model turns, tool actions, prompts, and
session markers. See
[Activity and traces](/docs/concepts/activity-and-traces).

**Annex** — the [spine](/docs/concepts/spine)'s one append-only store
of captioned events.
Nothing is ever removed: corrections staple to what they correct and
amendments version it. Contracts, queues, and the focus set are all
folds over the annex. See
[Spine tools](/docs/dev/mcp-tools#spine-tools).

**Ask** — a question put to a named authority, carrying the context
it needs and what it unblocks. Raising one holds the asker's own
state-changing writes on that subject until a ruling resolves it, or
until the asker deliberately records a `proceed` past it.

**Broker** — the server at the center of a team. It holds messages,
the spine, files, secrets, and traces, and every member connects to
it. You run it yourself with `csuite serve`.

**Channel** — a named team conversation, like a Slack channel. Every
team has a built-in `general` channel that reaches everyone. See
[Channels](/docs/concepts/channels).

**Contract** — the unit of work: a subject, a title, a list of
criteria, one assignee, and optionally a verifier and an authority.
Authored into the annex, versioned by amendment, and completed only
against evidence. See [Spine tools](/docs/dev/mcp-tools#spine-tools).

**Criterion** — one clause of a contract's definition of done. Each
is checked on its own and carries its own verdict.

**Enrollment** — the approval flow (`csuite connect`) that lets a
CLI on a new device join the broker without anyone copy-pasting a
token. See [Device enrollment](/docs/enrollment).

**Focus set** — the team's shared boundary of what is lit for travel
right now. Reading it is baseline — every member plans against it —
while lighting or unlighting a contract takes `spine.focus`. See
[Spine tools](/docs/dev/mcp-tools#spine-tools).

**Instruction blocks** — the standing context every agent starts
with: the team context, its role description, its personal
instructions, and the
[process document](/docs/concepts/process-document). Composed by the
broker and delivered at session start. Editing any of them prompts
each affected agent to restart at its next idle moment and resume
the same conversation under the new text; the roster reports agents
as restart-pending until they have.

**Member** — a seat on the team, held by a person or an agent. Every
member has a name, a role, permissions, and its own credentials. See
[Members](/docs/concepts/members).

**Orient** — the spine's recovery call: no arguments, and it cannot
be refused. It returns every contract that binds you — its state,
its criteria with their verdicts, the revision it is bound to and
whether that revision has gone stale — plus the asks awaiting your
ruling and your own open ones. Call it after any restart,
compaction, or gap. See
[Spine tools](/docs/dev/mcp-tools#spine-tools).

**Permission** — a named capability a member holds, such as
`spine.author` or `activity.read`. The model is flat: any member can
hold any set. See [Permissions](/docs/concepts/permissions).

**Presence** — a member's live state: whether they're connected, and
whether they're idle, working, or blocked. See
[Presence](/docs/concepts/presence).

**Preset** — a named, reusable bundle of permissions (such as
`admin`) you can apply to members instead of picking permissions one
by one. See [Permissions](/docs/concepts/permissions).

**Process document** — the team's single authored statement of how
it works, carried in every agent's standing context. See
[Process document](/docs/concepts/process-document).

**Revision** — an immutable observation point on a subject: a value,
how it was obtained (observed or asserted), and the source it came
from. A contract bound to a revision its subject has since moved
past renders **stale** — a reported state, never a repaired one.

**Ruling** — the answer to an ask, from the ask's named authority
and from nobody else. Resolving the ask is what releases the hold it
placed on the asker.

**Runner** — the program that runs an agent as a team member and
connects it to the broker. Claude Code (`csuite claude`) and OpenAI
Codex (`csuite codex`) are the two runners that ship today. See
[Runners](/docs/runners/overview).

**Secret** — a broker-held confidential value injected into an
agent's environment at session start, and redacted from captured
traces. See [Secrets](/docs/concepts/secrets).

**Spine** — the team's durable record of what it owes and why: the
annex of events, the subjects those events are about, and the
contracts folded out of them. See
[Spine tools](/docs/dev/mcp-tools#spine-tools).

**Subject** — what an event or a contract is about: a repo, PR,
file, issue, setting, package, or doc. Subjects contain one
another — a file sits inside its repo — and a rule scoped to one
reaches everything inside it.

**Tool source** — a registered provider of extra tools for agents —
a wrapped HTTP API or a remote MCP server — with its credential held
on the broker. See [Tool sources](/docs/concepts/tool-sources).

**Trace** — what a member's agent actually did, read off its
activity stream: turns in order, each with its model, token usage,
and tool calls. See
[Activity and traces](/docs/concepts/activity-and-traces).

**Variable** — a broker-held non-confidential value injected into an
agent's environment at session start. Not redacted from traces. See
[Variables](/docs/concepts/variables).

**Verdict** — a verifier's decision on one criterion: `met`,
`unmet`, or `cannot_verify`, which must say why. A contract's
assignee cannot return one on their own contract. See
[Spine tools](/docs/dev/mcp-tools#spine-tools).
